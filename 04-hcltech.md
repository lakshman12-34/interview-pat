## 1. Tracks and entry pay

### AMP: Ascend, Momentum, Polaris

The main campus and off-campus track for B.E./B.Tech. Official campus page.

| Tier | Pay | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Ascend** | ₹4.5 LPA. ₹3.75 LPA at the "New Vistas" locations: Lucknow, Nagpur, Vijayawada, Madurai | Everyone who clears the process |
| **Momentum** | ₹6.0 LPA | Only the **top 10% of Ascend clearers**, after extra interviews, hackathons or tests |
| **Polaris** | ₹10.0 LPA | Same as Momentum |

- Students from non-Primus colleges and off-campus hires must finish compulsory training and a test before joining. Clearing that test can upgrade an Ascend offer.

### Elite Engineers

- **IITs and NITs only.**
- **Areas:** AI, semiconductors and silicon, data engineering, physical AI and robotics, quantum.
- **Pay starts at ₹18 LPA** (official). FACE Prep (May 2026) gives an "Elite AI" band of ₹18–22 LPA.

### TechBee (after Class 12)

- 12 months of training, then a full-time job.
- **Starting pay:** ₹2.40 LPA (Digital Process Support) or ₹2.75 LPA (IT Services), from the official FAQ.
  - Older sources give ₹1.7–2.2 LPA. These are out of date.
- **Training stipend:** reported as ₹10,000 a month (search snippet from hcltechbee.com, page not opened).

### Other tracks

- **Graduate Trainee** (BA, BBA, B.Com, BCA, B.Sc, B.Pharma): pay not found.
- **MBA/PGDM, CA, Law and PhD tracks** also exist.

## 2. Eligibility

### AMP

- **Degree:** engineering in any branch, full-time only.
- **Marks:** **70% or more in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation**, or 65% at New Vistas locations (official).
- **Batch:** FY27 intake is the 2026 graduates. Campus visits ran from July 2025.

The rules below come from third parties and conflict with each other:

| Rule | FACE Prep (May 2026) | Wisdomland (updated Sep 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Backlogs | No active backlogs at interview | At most 1 active backlog |
| Education gap | Up to 1 year after Class 12; none during the degree | — |
| Re-applying | Wait 6 months | — |

### TechBee

- **Batch:** Class 12 passed in 2025 or 2026.
- **Class 12 marks needed (depends on the board):**
  - 75%: Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, NIOS.
  - 70%: CBSE, ISC, Karnataka, Odisha.
  - 65% or 60%: other states.
- **Maths:** Maths or Business Maths at 60% or more is needed for IT roles.

## 3. Selection process

**AMP (official):**
1. Cognitive test
2. Coding/technical test
3. Versant spoken-English test (only for those who clear steps 1 and 2)
4. Technical interview
5. HR interview
6. Top 10% only: further assessment for Momentum or Polaris

**TechBee:** registration → HCL CAT (quant, logical, language) → Versant → face-to-face interview.

## 4. Online test pattern

**Official AMP page (current, 2026 batch):**

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Cognitive: numerical, verbal, reasoning | Not stated | About 90 min |
| Coding / technical | Not stated | 60–90 min |
| **Versant** (speaking and listening) | Not stated | Pass mark **46–56** (B1 level) |

**Older pattern from prep sites** (PrepInsta Feb 2025; placementpreparation.io, labelled "2026"):

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Quant | 15 | 15 min |
| Logical | 15 | 15 min |
| Verbal | 15 | 15 min |
| Computer fundamentals | 30 | 30 min |
| Coding | 2 | 20 min |
| **Total** | **77** | **95 min** |

- **The official AMP format is newer** and replaces the 77-question pattern for the 2026 batch.
- **Negative marking:** none (PrepInsta, placementpreparation.io).
- **Adaptive:** yes, per PrepInsta. placementpreparation.io doesn't say.
- **Coding languages:** C, C++, Java, Python.
- **Cut-offs:** only the Versant score (46–56) is published.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- **Technical:**
  - Coding in C, C++, Java or Python.
  - Data structures: arrays, linked lists, trees, sorting.
  - OOP, operating systems, DBMS (for example triggers), basic networking.
  - A deep dive into your project.
- **HR:** introduce yourself, willingness to relocate or work shifts, strengths, career plans.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **Campus drives** start in July. The FY27 drive is reported as complete (HRKatha, 30 Sep 2026).
- **Primus campuses:** 60+ partner colleges that get hiring priority and a built-in curriculum.
- **Off-campus:** click "Apply now" on hcltech.com/careers/campus-hiring, which leads to registration at freshers.hcltech.com.
- **Another assessment route:** the official page also promotes "Campus Naukri Young Turks 26".

## 7. Hiring volume

- **FY26:** 11,744 freshers (HCLTech's Q4 FY26 investor release).
- **FY27:** about 11,000, similar to FY26 (HRKatha, 30 Sep 2026; ThePeoplesBoard, 1 Oct 2026).

## 8. Conflicts and gaps

- **Conflicts:** backlog and education-gap rules. There is no official statement.
- **Medium confidence:** per-section question counts. They date from 2025 and may not match the 90-minute official cognitive test.
- **Not found:** Graduate Trainee pay.

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Marks first.** The 70% cut-off is the highest of the 12 companies. Lyra's eligibility rules should flag students below it early.
- **Aptitude:** about 90 minutes of numerical, verbal and reasoning.
- **Coding:** 60–90 minutes in C, C++, Java or Python.
- **Spoken English:** Versant B1 (46–56). Lyra has no spoken-English practice today.
- **Interview:** technical and HR. The AI mock interview covers this.
