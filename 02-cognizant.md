## 1. Tracks and entry pay

Cognizant's official GenC page names only "GenC" and gives no pay. All the figures below are from prep sites.

| Track (role) | CTC | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **GenC** (Programmer Analyst Trainee) | ₹4.0 LPA | PrepInsta, GUVI |
| **GenC Pro** (Programmer Analyst) | ₹5.4 LPA | PrepInsta |
| **GenC Next** (Programmer Analyst) | ₹6.75 LPA | PrepInsta, GUVI (Sep 2026) |
| GenC Elevate | ₹4.5–5.4 LPA | Unstop |

- GUVI (15 Sep 2026) says **GenC Elevate is not a named track** in the current 2026 brochure.
- Unstop also reports "GenC Next ₹12 L+". No other source agrees, so treat it as an outlier.
- Pay for Analyst Trainee (the 3-year-degree route): not found.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Degrees (engineering, 2026 batch):** B.E./B.Tech and M.E./M.Tech; PrepInsta adds MCA and M.Sc CS/IT. The Jan 2026 drive excluded branches such as Leather, Food and Fashion Technology.
- **Marks:**
  - GenC and GenC Pro: 60% throughout Class 10, Class 12, UG and PG.
  - GenC Next: sources conflict. GUVI says 70%; placementpreparation.io says 60% or 6 CGPA.
- **Backlogs:** none active at the time of the test.
- **Education gap:** at most 2 years.
- **Age:** 18–30 (placementpreparation.io).
- **3-year-degree route:** an off-campus Analyst Trainee drive in Sep 2026 was open to 2025 and 2026 graduates of BCA, B.Sc, BA, B.Com, BBA, B.Voc and BMS.

## 3. Selection process

**Official page:** application → recruiter screening → tests (communication, aptitude and/or technical) → interview(s) → result.

**What candidates report:**
1. Registration on Superset
2. Communication test
3. Aptitude test
4. Technical test
5. Technical / subject-expert interview
6. Track decided: GenC, GenC Pro or GenC Next
7. Letter of intent

## 4. Online test pattern

PrepInsta, 2025–26 cycle. Not adaptive, no negative marking.

| Round | Content | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Communication** | AI-scored: reading, grammar, comprehension, listening, **speaking** | 60 |
| **Aptitude** | 30 quant questions (30 min) + **4 game-based tasks** (50 min) | 80 |
| **Technical** | 2 coding + 2 SQL + either 1 web task or 10 cloud MCQs (depends on cluster) | 120 |
| **Interview** | Technical and HR together | 30–45 |

**Technical clusters (you code in one language):**
1. Java + SQL + HTML/CSS/JS
2. Python + SQL + cloud basics
3. C# + SQL + HTML/CSS/JS

**How the track is decided:** from your technical test and interview performance.

**Other reported patterns (they conflict):**
- placementpreparation.io: Communication 60 Q / 58 min; Aptitude 80 Q / 100 min; Technical 3 Q in 105–120 min.
- Unstop (Dec 2024, older): 135 minutes in total.

**Cut-offs:** not found.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- Depth in the language you chose, including OOP.
- SQL queries and DBMS.
- Your projects, certifications and resume.
- HR: willingness to relocate and work shifts.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **2026 batch:** on-campus drives ran until Jan 2026; the Superset deadline was 6 Jan 2026.
- **3-year-degree off-campus drive:** closed 14 Sep 2026.
- **Joining** happens in batches based on business demand, so students may wait a long time for a joining date.
- **Portals:** Superset (app.joinsuperset.com) and careers.cognizant.com. Students track their status on the GenC candidate platform.
- **2027-batch dates:** not confirmed.

## 7. Hiring volume

- **2025:** about 20,000 entry-level graduates. CEO Ravi Kumar said he expects more in 2026 (PeopleMatters, 2 Jun 2026).
- **2026 plan:** about 25,000. Free Press Journal reports CFO Jatin Dalal confirming this. Unstop gives 24,000–25,000.

## 8. Conflicts and gaps

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Spoken English** (listening and speaking). Lyra has no spoken-English practice today.
- **Quant:** 30 questions in 30 minutes.
- **Game-based puzzles:** 4 games. Lyra has none today.
- **Coding plus SQL** in Java, Python or C#. SQL practice matters here.
- **Interview:** combined technical and HR. The AI mock interview covers this.
