## 1. Tracks and entry pay

| Track | Pay | Source |

- A later posting (placement-officer, Apr 2026) splits SP L1 as ₹10 L plus ₹1 L joining bonus, and DSE as ₹6.25 L plus ₹75,000 joining bonus. The two sources agree once the bonus is included.
- Older figures (PrepInsta, FACE Prep): SP ₹9.5 L, DSE ₹6.25 L, Power Programmer ₹9.5 L.
- **InfyTQ is now part of Infosys Springboard.** Its certificate is reported to give a preferred route to the SP track (FACE Prep).

## 2. Eligibility

**Systems Engineer:**
- **Degrees:** B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc.
- **Marks:** 60% in Class 10 and 12, and 6.0 CGPA in the degree (Oct 2026 posting; FACE Prep). PrepInsta says 65% or 6.5.
- **Backlogs:** none active.
- **Education gap:** at most 2 years.

**SP and DSE:**
- **Branches:** CS, IT, AI/ML/data science, cybersecurity, ECE, EEE and related branches.
- **Marks:** 6.8 CGPA (or 65%) in the degree, and 60% in Class 10 and 12 (PrepInsta).
- **Batches:** the Apr 2026 drive was for 2024 and 2025 graduates.
- **Repeat rule:** you cannot apply if you took part in Infosys recruitment in the last 6 months.

## 3. Selection process

| Track | Steps |
|---|---|
| **SE** | Online test → technical interview → HR interview (sometimes combined) |
| **SP / DSE** | Online coding test → in-person assessment and technical interview (about 3 hours) → HR |

## 4. Online test patterns

### Systems Engineer

Sectional cut-offs apply. No negative marking.

| Section | Questions | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical Ability | 10 | 35 |
| Logical Reasoning | 15 | 25 |
| Verbal Ability | 20 | 20 |
| Pseudocode | 5 | 10 |
| Puzzle Solving | 4 | 10 |
| English Grammar (some drives) | 5 | 10 |
| English Writing / essay (some drives) | 1 | 10 |

- **Total:** about 100 minutes, or 120 with the English parts. The Oct 2026 posting for the 2027 batch says 120 minutes, including grammar and a short essay.
- **Adaptive?** Sources conflict. PrepInsta says yes; FACE Prep says no.

### SP / DSE

- **3 coding questions in 3 hours:** 1 easy, 1 medium (greedy), 1 hard (dynamic programming).
- **Languages:** C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript.
- **Cut-offs:** sectional and overall (PrepInsta).
- **How the track is decided:** prep sites say about 2 or more questions solved leads to the SP interview, and about 1 leads to DSE. Not verified.

### HackWithInfy (2027 batch)

- Registration opened 6 Feb 2026, through college placement officers and an Infosys survey form.
- Round 1: a 3-hour coding test. Round 2: 3 hours, for the top 100 only. Then a grand finale.
- How results map to Power Programmer, SP or DSE: not officially confirmed.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- **SE:** C++, Java or Python; DBMS and SQL; operating systems; networking; a deep dive into your final-year project.
- **SP:** heavy on data structures and algorithms, with live coding.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **2026-batch SE:** campus registration ran Sep–Oct 2025 (one deadline was 19 Sep 2025), with the test taken in person.
- **SP/DSE off-campus:** deadline 13 Apr 2026.
- **2027-batch SE:** registration is open now through surveys.infosysapps.com (postings dated 1–2 Oct 2026).
- **Other routes:** career.infosys.com and Infosys Springboard.
- **Training:** new joiners train at Mysuru for 5–6 months (FACE Prep).

## 7. Hiring volume

- **FY26:** more than 20,000 freshers.
- **FY27:** "at least 20,000" planned, per CFO Jayesh Sanghrajka (Outlook Business, 24 Apr 2026).

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **SE:**
  - Maths (10 questions in 35 minutes; few questions, but hard), logical reasoning, verbal and pseudocode.
  - Puzzles.
  - A short essay.
- **SP/DSE:** 3 coding problems in 3 hours, including greedy and dynamic programming. This is much harder than the SE track.
- **Interview:** the AI mock interview covers technical questions and a project deep-dive.
