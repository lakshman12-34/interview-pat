## Important

The official careers pages say very little about diploma and ITI pay or tests. Most figures below come from job-notice sites that reprint campus or agency notices. **Tata Electronics has no dedicated Diploma Engineer Trainee (DET) programme.**

## 1. Roles open to diploma and ITI freshers

- **Shop-floor jobs on the iPhone and component lines:**
  - Titles: Technician, Junior Technician, Technician Trainee, Production Trainee, Assembly Line Operator.
  - Open to: 10th, 12th, ITI (any trade), diploma (any stream) and degree holders.
  - Mostly at Hosur (Tamil Nadu) and Narsapura/Kolar (Karnataka).
- **Production Trainee (NAPS), Kolar:** an 11-month apprenticeship, women only, 1,300 openings (Apr 2025).
- **"Trainee (Freshers) – NATS" walk-ins** at Tata Electronics Products & Solutions, Mahindra World City, Chengalpattu:
  - Engineering or diploma holders who passed in 2023–2026, with no arrears.
  - Departments: TEC (AE/ME/PE).
- **GET (B.E./B.Tech only):** aptitude test → 3-day selection boot camp → week-long training boot camp → internship with evaluation. One undated listing quotes about ₹7 LPA.
- **Jagiroad (Assam, chip assembly and test plant):** not a fresher route.
  - The Feb 2026 walk-ins asked for at least 3 years of experience.
  - Machinist needs 2–5 years; Training Technician 1–5 years.

## 2. Pay and benefits

| Route (date of source) | Pay | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Production Trainee NAPS, Kolar (Apr 2025) | Diploma (STEM) ₹15,000 a month; non-STEM ₹13,500; HSC/ITI ₹12,500–13,500 | Hostel with meals, shuttle, ₹3 L medical insurance, 2-week training |
| Technician, Hosur, women aged 18–34 (Oct 2025 drive) | CTC ₹19,128 a month; ₹14,200 in hand | 3 free meals, girls' hostel, transport, PF/ESI, uniform, shoes |
| Technician Trainee, Hosur (Apr 2026 drive at Govt ITI Sultanpur) | ₹18,800 a month | PF/ESIC, canteen, uniform, Diwali bonus, medical insurance |
| Junior Technician, Hosur, 500 posts (Sep 2026) | ₹19,000 CTC (₹14,000–15,000 in hand) | PF, ESI, food, room, transport |
| NATS Trainee, Chengalpattu | Apr 2026 listing: ₹15,000 + ₹1,000 attendance bonus. The Sep 2026 listing says only "best in industry". | Free food, transport, uniform |

**Pay after the trainee becomes permanent:** not found. The NAPS listing only says the trainee may be made permanent.

## 3. Eligibility

- **Age (varies by drive):**
  - 18–34/35 for the women technician drives.
  - 18–27 for the Apr 2026 technician trainee drive.
  - 18–20 for an older 12th-pass women's drive (undated booklet, probably 2023).
- **Gender:**
  - Many drives are women-only (Apr 2025 NAPS, Oct 2025 Hosur).
  - The Sep 2026 Junior Technician notice says "mostly female".
  - The Apr 2026 Sultanpur drive was open to men and women.
- **Marks cut-off:** not found for shop-floor routes.
- **Physical or medical rules:** not found.

## 4. Selection process

| Route | Steps |
|---|---|
| Women operator/technician drives, Hosur (undated booklet) | Psychometric test → knowledge test → practical test → one-to-one HR talk. Done in 4–5 hours with the result the same day, then 12 days of training at Hosur. |
| ITI campus drive (Apr 2026) | Written test → walk-in interview → document check |
| Junior Technician (Sep 2026) | Questions on technical troubleshooting, lean manufacturing and basic electronics → HR |
| NATS Trainee, Chengalpattu | Walk-in interview |

**Written-test sections, number of questions and time:** not found.

## 5. Locations and hiring volume

- **Locations:** Hosur, Narsapura/Kolar, Chengalpattu, Jagiroad, Dholera. Dholera roles on the careers portal are mostly for experienced staff.
- **Volume:** Tata Electronics added 15,000 workers between Nov 2025 and Apr 2026, mainly at Hosur. Two sources disagree on the total:
  - Communications Today (30 Apr 2026): about 75,000 across the whole company.
  - Bridge Chronicle (29 Nov 2025): Hosur alone growing from 60,000 to 75,000.
  - Step 1 found a separate report of "+20,000 at Hosur, taking it to 40,000" (Manufacturing Today).
- **A diploma-only hiring number for 2026:** not found.
- **The NATS route also takes graduates:** 28 B.Tech women from one college got NATS offers for Hosur (May 2026).

## 6. How students apply

- Hiring runs **all year**, through mass drives at Govt ITIs, polytechnics and colleges in many states.
- Many drives are run by **staffing partners**. For example, a TeamLease job ID is quoted for the Junior Technician role.
- **Official portal:** careers.tataelectronics.com. Searching it for "diploma" returned 30 jobs, mostly experienced technician roles at Jagiroad and Vemagal.

## 7. Conflicts and gaps

- **Medium confidence:** shop-floor pay and eligibility. Several 2025–26 notices broadly agree.
- **Low confidence:** the selection steps and test.
- **Conflict:** the total headcount figures.
- **Not found:**
  - An official diploma trainee programme.
  - Pay after training.
  - A marks cut-off.
  - The written-test pattern.

## 8. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Basic aptitude and a psychometric test.** The pattern isn't published, so Lyra should say "pattern not published".
- **Trade and subject knowledge:** basic electronics, troubleshooting, lean manufacturing.
- **Practical test:** hands-on work. Lyra can't practise this; show it as a checklist.
- **HR talk:** willingness to relocate to Hosur or Kolar, shift work and hostel life.
- **Show the eligibility clearly on the card:** age limits, women-only drives and location.
