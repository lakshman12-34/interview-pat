## 1. Tracks and entry pay

All campus hiring runs under **Exceller** (official). **The coding round decides your track.**

| Track | Grade | Pay | How you get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Analyst** | A4 | ₹4.25 LPA | Clear all rounds without fully solving a coding question |
| **Analyst Star** | A4(P) | ₹5.75 LPA | Fully solve 1 coding question |
| **Senior Analyst** | A5 | ₹7.5 LPA | Solve both coding questions |

Sources: PrepInsta (2026), FACE Prep (Aug 2026), a GeeksforGeeks 2026-batch experience.

Other figures that conflict:
- FACE Prep (May 2026): Analyst ₹4.0–4.5 L, Senior Analyst ₹6.5–7.5 L.
- placementpreparation.io: ₹4.00 L base.
- GUVI: the March 2026 off-campus opening paid ₹4.0 L.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Degrees:** B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc (CS/IT).
- **Marks:** **60% in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation.**
  - FACE Prep (May and Aug 2026) and FreshersNow (May 2026) say 60%.
  - GeeksforGeeks (Nov 2025, older) says 65%.
- **Backlogs:** none active at the test.
- **Education gap:** not found. Sources say it varies by drive.
- **Batches:** the 2026 batch for on-campus. FreshersNow lists 2025, 2026 and 2027 for off-campus roles.

## 3. Selection process

**Official FAQ:** online aptitude → coding/technical test → group discussion (some roles only) → technical interview → HR interview.

**What a 2026-batch candidate reported (GeeksforGeeks, Oct 2025):**
1. **Communication round** on a separate day: MCQs plus spoken English.
2. **Four days later, on Cocubes:** technical and pseudocode MCQs, 2 compulsory coding questions, game-based tests and behavioural questions.
3. **One offline interview** with technical and HR questions mixed.

Most stages eliminate candidates.

## 4. Online test pattern

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Technical MCQs + pseudocode (DBMS, networks, cloud, OOP, OS) | 40 | 40–50 min |
| English communication (grammar, reading; spoken English in some drives) | 30 | 30 min |
| **Game-based test** (from a pool of ~24 games, e.g. Motion, Digit, Switch, Grid) | 4 games | 20–30 min |
| Behavioural / "PowerSkills" | Not stated | 20–25 min |
| **Coding** | 2 | 45 min |

- **Not adaptive** (PrepInsta).
- **Negative marking:** sources conflict. PrepInsta (2026) says yes; placementpreparation.io says no.
- **Coding languages:** sources conflict.
  - PrepInsta and placementpreparation.io: C, C++, Java, Python.
  - FACE Prep (Aug 2026, newer): Python was not allowed in recent drives.
- **Cut-offs (placementpreparation.io):** Technical 60%, English 65%, coding at least 50% of test cases, games a qualifying score.

### New AI-era test (likely for the 2027 batch)

FACE Prep (Aug 2026) describes a new order:
1. AI-scored English (listening, speaking, reading, writing)
2. Technical MCQs with AI-literacy and data-structure questions
3. Debugging in C, C++ or Java
4. AI-assisted coding
5. Game-based tests

GUVI (24 Sep 2026, the newest source) says this is the **2027-batch** pattern, so it applies to the season that is starting now. Not confirmed officially.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- Projects and resume; OOP; DBMS/SQL; operating systems; networks; AI and its impact.
- **HR:** introduce yourself, why Capgemini, strengths and weaknesses, a difficult time you got through, relocation, learning new technology.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **On-campus:** through the college placement cell (official). The 2026 batch was tested around Oct 2025.
- **Off-campus:** the job search on capgemini.com/in-en/careers. One Exceller opening closed on 31 Mar 2026 (GUVI).

## 7. Hiring volume

- **2025:** Capgemini India planned **40,000–45,000 hires, 60–65% of them freshers** (DQ India, 6 Aug 2025).
- **2026:** not found. Only blogs repeat the 45,000 figure.

## 8. Conflicts and gaps

- Official pages give only the general process.
- **High confidence:** the three tracks (₹4.25 / 5.75 / 7.5 L) and the five-part test. Several sources and a 2026-batch candidate account agree.
- **Low confidence:** negative marking and whether Python is allowed. Sources conflict.
- **Unconfirmed:** the new AI-era test for the 2027 batch.
- **Not found:** the education-gap rule and the 2026 hiring number.

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Coding:** 2 problems in 45 minutes. Each one solved raises the pay track, so this is the most valuable part. Practise in C, C++ or Java, because Python may not be allowed.
- **Technical MCQs and pseudocode:** DBMS, networks, cloud, OOP and operating systems.
- **English:** grammar and reading, plus spoken English in some drives.
- **Game-based puzzles:** 4 games. Lyra has none today.
- **Debugging and AI literacy** for the 2027 batch.
- **Interview:** one combined technical and HR round. The AI mock interview covers this.
