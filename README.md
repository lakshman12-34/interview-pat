## What we learned

1. **IT companies now test spoken English.** Cognizant, Accenture, LTM (SVAR) and HCLTech (Versant) all have a spoken-English test, mostly scored by AI. Capgemini is adding one for the 2027 batch. Wipro and LTM also ask for a written essay.
2. **Game-based tests are common.** Cognizant (4 games), Accenture (3–5 games) and Capgemini (4 games from a pool of about 24) use short puzzle games in place of part of the aptitude test.
3. **Coding decides the pay track.** At most IT companies, the coding round decides which salary band you get:
   - TCS: the Advanced section decides Ninja vs Digital vs Prime.
   - Capgemini: 0, 1 or 2 coding questions solved gives ₹4.25 L, ₹5.75 L or ₹7.5 L.
   - Accenture: the coding score decides ASE vs AASE.
   - Infosys: SE vs DSE vs SP.
   - Cognizant: GenC vs GenC Pro vs GenC Next.
   - HCLTech: the top 10% of Ascend clearers are offered Momentum or Polaris.
4. **Marks cut-offs are going up.** HCLTech asks for 70%, LTM moved from 60% to 65% for the 2027 batch, and some Accenture postings ask for 65%.
5. **Most IT tests have no negative marking.** This is true for TCS, Cognizant, HCLTech, Wipro (MCQs) and Accenture. Sources conflict for Capgemini.
6. **IT fresher hiring is flat or falling; manufacturing is growing.**
   - Wipro set no FY27 target, and TCS has made 25,000 offers so far against 44,000 hires last year.
   - Tata Electronics added 15,000 workers in six months.
7. **Diploma students are hired by manufacturing companies, and their process is different:**
   - a short aptitude test plus subject questions, often with a practical test;
   - age limits, a medical test, and sometimes women-only or men-only drives;
   - an apprenticeship or training year before a permanent job.
8. **Manufacturing companies don't publish their test patterns.** Question counts and timings for Tata Electronics, L&T, Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors could not be found. Lyra should say "pattern not published", not invent numbers.
9. **Two companies changed their names:**
   - LTIMindtree became **LTM Limited** (announced 11 Feb 2026).
   - Tata Motors was split on 1 Oct 2025: **Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd** for cars, and **Tata Motors Ltd** for commercial vehicles.
10. **Two step-1 figures were corrected by the deeper research:**
    - Wipro hired 7,500 freshers in FY26, not 10,000–12,000.
    - L&T's DET pay is ₹2.5–2.97 L during training. The ₹3.2 L figure comes from one unverified site.

## How to read these files

- **Every figure has a date and a source.** Official company pages come first, then news reports quoting executives, then prep sites (PrepInsta, FACE Prep, GeeksforGeeks, GUVI, PapersAdda, placementpreparation.io), then candidate accounts and job-notice sites.
- **"Official"** means the company's own page or a college notice quoting it. **"Reported"** means a third party.
- **"Not found"** means we looked and could not find it. We did not guess.
- **Conflicts** are shown side by side, with the newer source named.
- **Confidence:**
  - **High:** official, or three or more sources agree.
  - **Medium:** two prep sources, or one detailed candidate account.
  - **Low:** one agency notice, or sources disagree.
