## 1. Tracks and entry pay

Wipro's official Early Careers page lists **Elite, Turbo, STAR and WILP**, but gives no pay.

| Track | Pay | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Elite NTH** (Project Engineer) | ₹3.5 LPA | FreshersNow (Jul 2026), PapersAdda (Oct 2026). FACE Prep (Aug 2026) says ₹3.5–4.0 L. FreshersNow reports a ₹25,000 bonus after 6 months. |
| **Turbo** | ~₹6.5 LPA | PapersAdda (Oct 2026), reported by candidates, not official. Officially limited to **46 premier institutes** (IIITs, NITs, top private colleges). |
| **STAR** | Not verified for 2026 | Officially for the **top 13 institutes**. Older snippets say about ₹10 L plus ₹1 L retention bonus. |
| **WILP** (BCA/B.Sc) | Stipend: ₹15,000 / 17,000 / 19,000 / 23,000 a month in years 1–4 | placement-officer.com (Jun 2026). Also a ₹75,000 joining bonus, an M.Tech sponsored by Wipro, and a 60-month service agreement. |

## 2. Eligibility

### Elite NTH

- **Degrees:** B.E./B.Tech, or 5-year integrated M.E./M.Tech, full-time.
- **Branches:** all except Fashion Technology, Textile, Agriculture and Food Technology.
- **Marks:** **60% (6.0 CGPA) in Class 10, Class 12 and the degree.** All sources agree.
- **Service agreement:** 12 months; ₹75,000 pro-rata if you leave early (FreshersNow).
- **Batches:** FreshersNow says 2025, 2026 and 2027; PapersAdda says 2025–26.

Sources conflict on backlogs and gaps:

| Rule | FreshersNow (Jul 2026) | FACE Prep (Aug 2026) and PapersAdda (Oct 2026) — newer |
|---|---|---|
| Backlogs | 1 allowed at the test stage | **Zero active backlogs** |
| Education gap | Up to 3 years between Class 10 and degree | **1 year or less** |

### Turbo

- 65% or 6.5 CGPA in the degree (PapersAdda).

### WILP

- **Degrees:** BCA, or B.Sc in CS, IT, Maths, Statistics, Electronics or Physics, with Maths as a core subject.
- **Marks:** 60% throughout.
- **Backlogs:** 1 allowed at the online test.
- **Gap:** 3 years or less from Class 10 to the start of the degree, and none during it.
- **Batch:** sources conflict. placement-officer (1 Jun 2026) says 2024 and 2025 graduates; placementpreparation.io (undated) says 2026.

## 3. Selection process

**Elite NTH:**
1. Registration
2. Online test
3. Business Discussion (technical and HR in one interview)
4. Letter of intent
5. Offer

**Turbo and WILP** add a voice test, and training before the offer.

## 4. Online test pattern

Elite NTH, on AMCAT, proctored.

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Quant | 16 | 16 min |
| Logical | 14 | 14–18 min |
| Verbal | 18–22 | 14–18 min |
| **Aptitude block total** | ~52 (FACE Prep says ~36) | **48 min** |
| Written communication (essay) | 1 | 20 min |
| Coding | 2 | 60 min |
| **Total** | | **128 min** |

- **Coding languages:** Java, C, C++, Python.
- **Not adaptive.** Each section has its own timer, and you cannot go back.
- **Negative marking:** none on MCQs. The essay loses marks after 5 spelling or grammar errors (placementpreparation.io).

**Cut-offs (sources conflict):**

| Section | placementpreparation.io (Dec 2024) | PrepInsta Turbo (2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Quant | 70th percentile | ~65% |
| Logical | 70th percentile | ~60% |
| Verbal | 75th percentile | ~60% |
| Essay | 80th percentile | ~70% |
| Coding | 80th percentile | ~50% |

**WILP test (80 min):** Verbal, Analytical and Quant at 20 questions each, plus a 20-minute written test.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- Data structures and programming basics, OOP, DBMS.
- A walkthrough of your project, and questions on the language you coded in.
- **HR:** relocation, shifts, and agreeing to the service agreement.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **Apply** at careers.wipro.com/elite or through the college placement officer. The test is free.
- **Typical cycle (reported by candidates, PapersAdda):**
  - Pre-registration: Aug–Sep
  - Campus drives: Sep–Jan
  - Off-campus drives: Feb–Apr
  - Offers: Mar–Jun
- **Current cycle: unclear.** FreshersNow (Jul 2026) shows a cycle open until Sep 2026, but a 21 Sep 2026 snippet (not opened) says the next cycle had not opened.
- **WILP:** last date was 30 Jun 2026 (placement-officer).

## 7. Hiring volume

- **FY26:** 7,500 freshers hired.
- **FY27:** the CHRO said there is **no target; hiring is "completely on demand"** (BusinessToday, 17 Apr 2026).
- Other sites claim **zero freshers joined in Q1 FY27**. The official Q1 FY27 earnings call (16 Jul 2026) doesn't mention freshers, so this is not verified.
- More than 250 people from the 2025 batch were still waiting to join in Jan 2026 (Outlook Business).

## 8. Conflicts and gaps

- Wipro publishes no official eligibility or pay. Everything except the track names comes from third parties.
- **High confidence:** ₹3.5 LPA, the 60% rule and the 128-minute structure (all sources agree).
- **Medium confidence:** Turbo ₹6.5 LPA, and the section question counts.
- **Conflicts:** backlog and gap rules; cut-offs.
- **Not found:** STAR pay; an FY27 hiring number.
- **Risk for students:** FY27 fresher hiring looks small and on demand, and onboarding delays have been reported.

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Aptitude:** quant, logical and verbal, about 48 minutes. You cannot go back between sections.
- **Essay:** 20 minutes. Spelling and grammar matter, because marks are lost after 5 errors.
- **Coding:** 2 problems in 60 minutes.
- **Interview:** one combined technical and HR round. The AI mock interview covers this.
