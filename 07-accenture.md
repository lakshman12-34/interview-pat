**Most important finding:** Accenture changed its campus test for the 2026 batch. The old aptitude sections were replaced with a behavioural test, puzzle games, technical MCQs, three coding tasks and an AI-scored spoken-English test.

## 1. Tracks and entry pay

Accenture's official site does not publish pay.

| Track | Pay | Breakdown / source |
|---|---|---|
| **Associate Software Engineer (ASE)** | ₹4.50 LPA | PrepInsta: ₹3.83 L fixed + ₹32,500 performance bonus + ₹25,000 joining bonus. FACE Prep (Aug 2026): band of ₹4.5–6.5 L. |
| **Advanced ASE (AASE, grade 11A)** | ₹6.50 LPA | PrepInsta: ₹5.41 L fixed + ₹82,500 performance pay + ₹26,500 joining bonus. FACE Prep: ₹6.5–9.0 L. |
| Packaged App Development Associate | ₹4,60,700 a year | PrepInsta, posting for the 2025 batch |

- **Your coding score decides ASE or AASE.**
- **Official site:** the India technology graduates page says applications for graduate and post-graduate roles are **currently closed**.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Degrees:** B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc (CS/IT). The Packaged App role excludes PGDM/MBA. One July 2026 listing also covers non-engineering roles for any graduate.
- **Marks (sources conflict):**

| Source | Rule |
|---|---|
| GeeksforGeeks (Jan 2026) | 65% or 6.5 CGPA in Class 10, Class 12 and degree |
| PrepInsta | 65% or 6.5 CGPA in the degree only |
| PapersAdda (Sep 2026) | About 65% |
| FACE Prep (May 2026) | 60% throughout |
| **FACE Prep (Aug 2026, newest)** | **Set per posting, not fixed** |

- **Backlogs:** none active at application or joining. All sources agree.
- **Education gap:** at most 1 year (FACE Prep). The Packaged App posting allows none: the B.Tech must be finished in 4 years.
- **Other:** up to 11 months of work experience. You must not have taken an Accenture test in the last 3 months (Packaged App posting).
- **Batches:** the 2025–26 campus season targeted the 2026 batch. Off-campus listings in July 2026 named the 2025, 2026 and 2027 batches.

## 3. Selection process (2026-batch campus drive)

1. Mock test
2. Behavioural and game-based test
3. Technical MCQs
4. Coding
5. Communication (spoken English) test
6. Combined technical + HR interview

Some older off-campus drives still use the earlier order: cognitive + technical → coding → communication → interview.

## 4. Online test pattern

| Section | **New pattern** (PrepInsta 2026) | Older pattern (FACE Prep May 2026; Packaged App drive) |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural / psychometric | 54 Q, 20 min | — |
| Cognitive | **3 games, 20 min** | 50 Q (English, critical reasoning, abstract reasoning) |
| Technical MCQ | 45 Q, 45 min (pseudocode, MS Office, networking, security, cloud) | 40 Q; cognitive + technical share 90 min |
| Coding | **3 Q, 60 min** | 2 Q, 45 min |
| Communication | About 20 Q, 30 min | 25–35 min; 63 Q in 6 sections (FACE Prep) or 30 Q (Packaged App) |
| **Total** | **125 Q, 175 min** | About 165–170 min |

- **Not adaptive** (PrepInsta). Other sources don't say.
- **Negative marking:** none (PrepInsta). PapersAdda confirms none for the games.
- **Cut-offs:** every section eliminates on its own. PrepInsta says about 70%; PapersAdda about 65% per section.
- **The games** (PapersAdda, 7 Sep 2026): Path Finder, Memory, Tower Sorting, Number Sense and Reaction Time, about 30–40 minutes in all. PrepInsta counts 3 games in 20 minutes.
- **The coding tasks in the 2026-batch drive** (a GeeksforGeeks write-up of an AASE campus drive, Oct–Dec 2025):
  1. A front-end task (HTML/CSS/JS)
  2. An SQL query using joins
  3. A back-end problem (sliding window)
  - Languages accepted: C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, SQL, .NET.
- **Communication test:** AI-scored spoken English covering sentence repetition, vocabulary, fluency, pronunciation and listening. It is taken from home. Several sources say you cannot be eliminated on it alone, but you must take it.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- Your final-year project and why you chose that tech stack, plus SQL query tuning.
- Data structures and algorithms, DBMS/SQL, OOP basics.
- **HR:** "Why Accenture?", relocation, a conflict situation, family background.
- AASE candidates get deeper technical questions.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **One campus drive (GeeksforGeeks):**
  - Registration: 17 Sep 2025
  - Test: 13 Oct 2025
  - Interview: 10 Nov 2025
  - Result: 3 Dec 2025
- An off-campus drive closed on 9 Jan 2026. Campus drives usually take 4–6 weeks from test to offer.
- A less reliable blog reports Accenture delaying joining dates in Aug 2026.
- **Portals:** campus candidates use indiacampus.accenture.com (MyZone); off-campus candidates use accenture.com/in-en/careers.

## 8. Conflicts and gaps

- **High confidence:**
  - The new test format (behavioural, games, technical, coding, communication). Three separate 2025–26 sources agree.
  - No active backlogs.
  - ₹4.5 LPA for ASE.
- **Medium:** AASE pay (₹6.5 L, or a ₹6.5–9.0 L band); exact question counts (3 coding Q in 60 min, or 2 in 45 min).
- **Low:** the marks cut-off (60–65%, possibly now per posting).
- **Not found:** an official test pattern, a fresher hiring number, and the 2027-batch timeline.

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Behavioural test:** 54 questions in 20 minutes. Lyra's soft-skills scenarios are related practice.
- **Game-based puzzles:** memory, path finding, number sense, reaction time. Lyra has none today.
- **Technical MCQs:** pseudocode, networking, security, cloud, MS Office.
- **Coding:** 3 tasks in 60 minutes (front-end, SQL joins, a back-end algorithm). The score decides ASE or AASE.
- **Spoken English:** sentence repetition, fluency, pronunciation. Lyra has no spoken-English practice today.
- **Interview:** combined technical and HR. The AI mock interview covers this.
