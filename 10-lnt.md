## 1. Roles

### DET (Diploma Engineer Trainee)

- Mainly for **L&T Construction**, recruited through the **L&T EduTech** portal.
- **Typical roles (official page):** site execution, safety and procurement, stores.
- **Training:** 12 months on the job (2025 and 2026 notices).
- **Branches in the 2025 notice:** Architecture, Automobile, Chemical, Civil, CS/IT, EEE, ECE, E&I, Environmental, EHS/Industrial Safety, Fire Safety, I&C, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Mining, Survey.
- **2026 notices** list Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, ECE, IT "and others".

### JET (Junior Engineer Trainee), L&T Heavy Engineering

- For **2026 pass-outs** with a 3-year diploma in **Mechanical or Electrical**.
- Locations: Surat, Vadodara, Mumbai.
- **Applications close 5 Oct 2026.**
- You can't apply if you took part in an earlier L&T campus process.

### LTTS DET 2026

- LTTS (L&T Technology Services) is a separate listed group company.
- 60% cut-off, 2026 pass-outs; branches include Mechatronics and Power Technology.
- Pay: "as per company standards".

### GET (Graduate Engineer Trainee)

- For B.E./B.Tech graduates.
- Official process: online test of subject knowledge and aptitude → personal interview → medical.
- Pay: not found from an official source.

## 3. Eligibility (DET 2026)

- **Diploma:** full-time, 3-year (6-semester), with **at least 60% aggregate**.
- **Attempts:** passed in the **first attempt**, with no past or current backlogs.
- **Not accepted:** sandwich and part-time courses.
- **Completion:** diploma finished by 30 Jun 2026.
- **Date of birth:** 1 Jul 2004 – 30 Jun 2008 (tnjobzone).
  - The 2025 cycle had conflicting ranges: 1 Jul 2003 – 30 Jun 2007 (jobsforyoutamizha) and 1 Jul 2002 – 30 Jun 2008 (tnjobzone).
- **Class 10/12 cut-off:** not in the DET notices. Unstop (undated) claims 65% in diploma and 60% in Class 10/12, which conflicts.
- **Medical:** fit as per L&T norms.
- **Gender:** no restriction.

## 4. Selection process

| Stage | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1. Online written test | Numerical, analytical, verbal and domain (subject) knowledge (2025 notice) |
| 2. Interview | Technical, plus HR |
| 3. Medical | L&T fitness standards |

**Reported test pattern (not official):**

| Source | Pattern |
|---|---|
| One candidate (GeeksforGeeks, May 2022) | Quant 10–15, English 15–20, Logical 10–15, core technical 20–25 MCQs; 90–120 min |
| Unstop (undated) | Technical aptitude 40–60 plus core 10–15 |

L&T publishes no official question count.

## 5. Locations and hiring volume

- DETs are posted to **project sites and offices across India**.
- **2026 DET number:** not found officially.
- **L&T Construction** says it visits 300 campuses and hires about **2,500 students a year** (all levels).
- **FY2023-24 annual report:**
  - 2,600+ GET/PGET hires, 30% of them women.
  - 1,600+ other trainees, including diploma engineers.

## 6. Season and how to apply

**Official DET calendar:**
1. Aug–Nov: L&T invites colleges
2. Dec–Feb: campus visits
3. Mar–May: pre-joining
4. July: joining

**Off-campus applications** go through lteforms.lntedutech.com:
- Reported 2026 DET windows closed on 5 Feb 2026 and 10 Apr 2026.
- The DET 2026 form now shows "Registrations closed".
- No application fee.

## 7. Conflicts and gaps

- **Medium-high confidence:** DET pay and eligibility. They match across the 2025 and 2026 cycles.
- **Low confidence:** the test pattern.
- **Conflicts:**
  - Date-of-birth ranges in 2025.
  - The stipend claims.
  - The Class 10/12 cut-off.
- **Not found:** pay after training, JET pay, GET pay, and the 2026 hiring number.

## 8. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Aptitude:** quant, English and logical, about 10–20 questions each.
- **Core subject MCQs:** 20–25 questions on your branch (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical and so on). This is the biggest part.
- **Interview:** technical questions on your branch, plus HR. Relocation to project sites matters.
- **Show the strict rules on the card:** first-attempt pass, no backlogs, the date-of-birth range, and the medical.
- **Deadlines:** JET 2026 closes 5 Oct 2026, so Lyra should show deadlines on the card.
