## 1. Tracks and entry pay

Source: an RGUKT Training & Placement notice dated 19 Feb 2026, which quotes tcs.com. GeeksforGeeks (updated 3 Mar 2026) shows the same table.

| Track | UG, 0–1 yr experience | PG, 0–1 yr experience | UG / PG, 1–2 yr experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prime** | ₹9.09–9.30 LPA | ₹11.59–11.80 LPA | ₹9.45–9.66 / ₹12.05–12.26 LPA |
| **Digital** | ₹7.09–7.30 LPA | ₹7.39–7.60 LPA | ₹7.50–7.72 / ₹7.82–8.04 LPA |
| **Ninja** | ₹3.40 LPA | ₹3.60 LPA | Not found |

Prep sites and a Sep 2026 job post give rounded figures: Ninja ₹3.36 L, Digital ₹7.0 L, Prime ₹9 L+ (about ₹11.5 L for PG). These are reported by candidates.

## 2. Eligibility

Official (T&P notice, Feb 2026). GeeksforGeeks agrees.

- **Degrees:** B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc/MS in any specialisation. Full-time courses only.
- **Marks:** at least 60% (or equal CGPA) in Class 10, Class 12, diploma, UG and PG.
- **Backlogs:** none pending when you take the test. The course must be finished in its normal duration.
- **Education gap:** at most 24 months overall, and every gap must be declared.
- **Work experience:** up to 2 years allowed.
- **Age:** 18–28.
- **Batches:** 2024, 2025 and 2026 graduates for the Feb–Mar 2026 and Aug–Sep 2026 drives.

## 3. Selection process

1. NQT online test
2. Technical interview
3. Managerial interview
4. HR interview
5. Offer

- FACE Prep lists only Technical and HR rounds.
- PapersAdda says test results come 15–20 days after the test, and the offer 10–15 working days after the interview.

## 4. Online test: TCS All India NQT

**190 minutes, 83 questions.**

| Part | Section | Questions | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Foundation** (everyone) | Numerical Ability | 20 | 25 |
| | Verbal Ability | 25 | 25 |
| | Reasoning Ability | 20 | 25 |
| **B. Advanced** (needed for Digital and Prime) | Advanced Quant + Reasoning | ~15 | 25 |
| | Advanced Coding | 2 (PrepInsta, Jul 2026) or 3 (FACE Prep, May 2026) | 90 |

- **Not adaptive.** No negative marking.
- **Each section has its own timer** and locks when time runs out.
- **Coding languages:** C, C++, Java, Python, Perl. FACE Prep also lists C#.
- **How the track is decided:** clearing Foundation only leads to Ninja. Clearing Foundation and Advanced leads to Digital or Prime.
- **Cut-offs:** TCS does not publish them. Prep sites say Digital and Prime need about 7.0+ CGPA (not official).

**A separate, paid test also exists: TCS iON NQT.**
- October cycle: applications close 7 Oct 2026, exam on 19 Oct 2026. Fee from ₹599.
- Pattern (Testbook): Verbal 24 Q / 30 min, Reasoning 30 / 50, Numerical 26 / 40, Programming Logic 10 / 15, and 2 coding tasks in 45 min.
- The score is valid for 2 years.
- Eligible batches conflict: Testbook says 2020–2026, PapersAdda says 2021–2027.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- **Technical:** basics of C, C++, Java or Python; OOP; arrays, linked lists, stacks and queues; DBMS and SQL; operating systems; your projects.
- **Prime:** deeper data structures and algorithms, plus a review of an AI or data project.
- **Managerial:** situation-based questions.
- **HR:** communication, willingness to relocate, knowledge of TCS.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **2026-batch drive:** registration 18 Feb–20 Mar 2026; tests from 10 Mar 2026.
- **September 2026 cycle:** applications 17 Aug–2 Sep 2026, open to 2024–26 batches.
- **Portal:** TCS NextStep (nextstep.tcsapps.com/indiacampus). Register under the "IT" category, then click **"Apply For Drive"**. Without that click the registration is incomplete.

## 7. Hiring volume

- **FY26:** more than 44,000 freshers hired.
- **FY27:** 25,000 offers made so far. CEO K Krithivasan said more depends on demand (BusinessToday, 13 Apr 2026).

## 8. Conflicts and gaps

- **Conflicts:**
  - Advanced Coding has 2 or 3 problems, depending on the source.
  - Ninja pay is given as ₹3.36 L or ₹3.40 L.
- **Not found:**
  - Official cut-off scores.
  - A separate hiring number for Ninja.

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Aptitude:** Numerical, Verbal and Reasoning sets, each timed at 25 minutes with the timer locking at the end.
- **Advanced aptitude:** harder quant and reasoning sets, for students aiming at Digital or Prime.
- **Coding:** 2–3 problems in 90 minutes. This part decides Digital or Prime.
- **Interviews:** three rounds (technical, managerial, HR). The AI mock interview covers technical and HR; managerial questions are situation-based.
