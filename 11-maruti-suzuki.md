## 1. Roles

### JET (Junior Engineer Trainee), Production

- **JET1** is for 3-year diplomas; **JET2** for 4-year diplomas.
- **Branches:** Mechanical, Mechatronics, Electrical, Paint Technology, Automobile, Tool & Die.
- **Plant:** Kharkhoda (Sonipat, Haryana).
- **Two 2026 cycles:**
  - 2026 batch: applications closed 11 Jan 2026 (Scribd copy of the notice).
  - "JET 26-27" off-campus for 2025 and 2026 pass-outs: closed 16 Sep 2026.

### Diploma Apprentice Trainee (Gujarat)

This route exists, because the JET 26-27 notice says current trainees can't apply for JET. Details not found.

### ITI routes

| Plant | Route |
|---|---|
| Gujarat (Hansalpur) | 12-month Apprentice; 36-month FTC (fixed-term contract) |
| Kharkhoda | FTE / FTE-K (fixed-term employment) |
| Manesar / Gurugram | Casual Worker (1 year); FTE-1 |

### GET (Graduate Engineer Trainee)

- B.E./B.Tech only.
- 65% in Class 10, 12 and B.E.
- Colour blindness or vision problems disqualify.

- **A separate JET training stipend:** not found. The JET figure is quoted as CTC.

## 3. Eligibility

### JET

- **Marks:** at least 60% (or 6.0 CGPA) in Class 10, Class 12 (if taken) and diploma.
- **Course:** full-time regular, passed in the first attempt, no active backlogs.
- **Age:** over 18 and under 23 at joining.
- **Batch:** 2025 or 2026.
- **Medical:** must pass.

### ITI

- **Marks:** Class 10 at least 40%; ITI at least 50% (Gujarat), NCVT or SCVT.
- **Age (varies by notice):**
  - 18–26 (Gujarat, Apr 2026); Kharkhoda up to 25 years 11 months.
  - 18 to 25 years 9 months (Jul 2026).
  - 18–24 (Gujarat, Sep 2026).
- **Physical:** no colour blindness, abnormal BMI or height, or deformity.
- **Gender (varies by drive):** Gujarat (Apr 2026) and Kharkhoda (Mar 2026) were men-only; Manesar (May 2026) was women-only.

## 4. Selection process

| Route | Stages |
|---|---|
| **JET** | Shortlist on marks, branch, pass-out year and age → online test (technical, aptitude and reasoning, psychometric) → technical and HR interview → medical |
| **ITI** (FTE, Casual Worker, Temporary Workman) | Online registration → written or technical test → interview → document check → medical before joining (some add a background check) |

**Reported written-test pattern** (hitbullseye, undated; role not stated, probably GET):

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Aptitude (10 verbal + 10 quant + 10 reasoning) | 30 | 30 min |
| Technical | 30 | 30 min |
| Psychometric | 50 | 12 min |
| **Total** | **110** | **72 min** |

- No negative marking.
- **A JET-specific pattern was not found.**

## 5. Locations and hiring volume

- **JET:** posted to Kharkhoda.
- **ITI:** Kharkhoda, Manesar, Gurugram and Hansalpur.
- **Volume:** individual ITI drives say "500+" posts (Jul 2026). Total 2026 hiring: not found.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **JET:**
  - Off-campus applications on maruti.app.param.ai, plus polytechnic placement cells.
  - The 2026 cycles ran around Dec–Jan and in Sep.
- **ITI:** rolling drives at Govt ITIs in UP, HP, Bihar, Rajasthan and West Bengal, run by manpower agencies, with registration on Zappyhire.
- **Fraud warning:** Maruti's official notice says all valid job information is only on its own website, and it never charges fees.

## 8. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Aptitude:** verbal, quant and reasoning, about 1 minute per question.
- **Technical:** subject questions for Mechanical, Electrical, Automobile and similar branches.
- **Psychometric test:** about 50 quick questions in 12 minutes. Lyra's soft-skills scenarios are related practice.
- **Interview:** technical and HR. The AI mock interview covers this.
- **Show the strict rules on the card:** age under 23 at joining, first-attempt pass, colour-vision and medical, and the ₹1.2 L bond.
