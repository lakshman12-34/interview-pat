## About the company split

The company demerged on **1 Oct 2025**:
- The passenger-vehicle business is now **Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd**.
- The commercial-vehicle business was renamed **Tata Motors Ltd** (Business Today, 24 Oct 2025).

The programmes below are listed on tatamotors.com, which does not say which company runs each one. **In Lyra, show it as "Tata Motors" with a note about the split.**

## 1. Routes

### Lakshya Program 2026 (official)

- **Only for 2026 diploma graduates from Government polytechnics.**
- **Branches:** Automobile, Electrical, Industrial Electronics, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Paint Technology.
- **Year 1:** Technician Apprentice.
- **From year 2:** permanent role as "Shop-floor Operating Engineer", "Engineer–Sales" or "Engineer–Customer Care".
- **Fully sponsored B.Tech** from year 2 (BITS Pilani work-integrated programme or similar).
- **Applications closed 30 Jan 2026.**

### Other routes

| Route | Who | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Diploma Technician Apprentice (NATS)** | Diploma holders | Example: Pune "Technical Apprentice", 1 year, 250+ posts, for 2024 pass-outs in Mech/Auto/Production/Metallurgy/Mechatronics. Closed 31 Dec 2025. |
| **Graduate Apprentice Trainee (NATS)** | B.E./B.Tech 2024/25 only | Pune and Sanand |
| **Kaushalya Earn & Learn** (with Quess) | 12th pass or 2-year ITI, age 18–23 | Earn a diploma in Mechatronics while working: 3 years for 12th pass, 2 years for ITI |
| **Trade Apprentice** (official) | 10th pass | 2 years. Pune, Sanand, Jamshedpur, Lucknow, Pantnagar, Dharwad. |
| **Saksham 2026** | 2024 diploma holders with 12–24 months of sales or service experience | Not a fresher route. ₹3.36 LPA plus a sponsored B.Tech. |

## 2. Pay

| Route (date) | Pay |
|---|---|
| Lakshya year-1 stipend and year-2 CTC | **Not found** |
| Diploma Technician Apprentice, Pune (Dec 2025) | ₹16,000 a month |
| Diploma Apprentice Trainee, Ahmedabad (Feb 2024, older) | ₹1.5 L a year, plus a company-sponsored B.Tech for those who qualify |
| Graduate Apprentice Trainee (Jun 2026) | ₹25,000 a month |
| Kaushalya (Sep 2026) | ₹17,350 + ₹1,500 attendance + ₹1,000 retention bonus |
| Kaushalya (Oct 2025 drive) | ₹14,649 + ₹2,500 attendance = ₹17,149 |

- **Legal NATS minimum from 1 Apr 2026:** ₹10,900 a month for diploma apprentices and ₹12,300 for graduate apprentices. The government pays 50% by direct transfer.
- **Benefits:** subsidised or free canteen, company transport, insurance, uniform and safety shoes.
- **CTC after the apprenticeship:** not found.

## 3. Eligibility

| Route | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Lakshya** | At least 60% from SSC onwards; no ATKT or backlog in any semester. Age and gender not stated. |
| **Diploma apprentice** | You must not have signed an earlier NATS contract. The 2024 Ahmedabad notice required 60% in Class 10 and diploma, age 18–24, men or women. |
| **Graduate Apprentice Trainee** | 60% in Class 10, Class 12 and B.E.; gap of at most 2 years; no backlogs |
| **Kaushalya** | Age 18–23; men and women |
| **Trade Apprentice** | SSC at least 60% (55% for SC/ST); at least 60% in Maths and Science; passed in one attempt |

## 4. Selection process

| Route | Stages / test |
|---|---|
| **Lakshya** (official) | Online test: **problem solving** (general aptitude and reasoning), **quantitative literacy** (basic maths), **conceptual understanding** (your subject), **general English**. Number of questions, time and later rounds are not stated. |
| Diploma Technician Apprentice | Written test on engineering basics → interview (edumetrics, general) |
| Graduate Apprentice Trainee | Shortlist → invitation to the selection process (not detailed) |
| Kaushalya | Screening on age and education → document check → skill counselling → plant allocation |

## 5. Locations and hiring volume

- **Plants:** Pune, Sanand, Jamshedpur, Lucknow, Pantnagar, Dharwad.
- **Kaushalya:** 1,000 seats at Pune (Sep 2026, agency notice).
- **Pune Technician Apprentice:** 250+ posts (Dec 2025).
- **Overall 2026 diploma hiring:** not found.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **Lakshya:** applications Dec–Jan on the Tata Motors portal.
- **Apprentices:** rolling, through TurboHire and the NATS portal.
- **Kaushalya:** rolling ITI and college drives run by Quess.

## 8. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Aptitude and reasoning** (problem solving).
- **Basic maths** (quantitative literacy).
- **Subject concepts:** Automobile, Electrical, Mechanical, Mechatronics and the other listed branches.
- **General English.**
- **Who can apply:** Lakshya is only for **Government polytechnic** students. Lyra should show this on the card, so students at private polytechnics are not misled.
- **Deadline:** Lakshya applications open in Dec–Jan, so Lyra should remind students in November.
