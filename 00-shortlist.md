# 00 · Step 1: Which companies hire freshers in large numbers?

Researched 3 Oct 2026. [← Back to README](README.md)

We first looked for companies that hire freshers **on campus in large numbers** in India. We found 16, in three groups. For diploma (polytechnic) students, the big recruiters are manufacturing companies. IT companies mostly hire B.E./B.Tech/MCA graduates.

---

## 1. IT services: the biggest campus recruiters (B.E./B.Tech, some MCA/B.Sc)

| # | Company | Freshers hired (latest public figure) | Main fresher programme |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **TCS** | ~44,000 in FY26. FY27 started with 25,000 offers, the lowest start since FY20. | TCS NQT (Ninja / Digital / Prime) |
| 2 | **Cognizant** | ~20,000 in 2025. The plan for 2026 is about 25,000. | GenC, GenC Pro, GenC Next |
| 3 | **Infosys** | 20,000+ in FY26, and at least 20,000 planned for FY27 | Systems Engineer and higher-paid tracks |
| 4 | **HCLTech** | 11,744 in FY26 (its own investor release) | AMP; TechBee (after Class 12) |
| 5 | **Wipro** | 7,500 in FY26. No FY27 target. *(Step 1 said 10,000–12,000; corrected in step 2.)* | Elite National Talent Hunt; WILP |
| 6 | **Capgemini** | Planned 40,000–45,000 India hires in 2025, 60–65% of them freshers. No 2026 figure. | Exceller |
| 7 | **LTIMindtree** (now LTM) | ~5,000 target in FY26; more than 6,000 in FY27 | Graduate Engineer Trainee |
| 8 | **Accenture** | Not published ("thousands" a year) | Associate Software Engineer |
| 9 | **Tech Mahindra** | Not published; restarted campus hiring in 2026 | Associate Software Engineer |
| 10 | Mid-size: **Mphasis, Hexaware, Persistent** | Not published (hundreds to low thousands) | Trainee / Associate roles |

---

## Trends

- **IT fresher hiring is shrinking**, except at Cognizant. TCS started FY27 with its lowest intake in six years, and the top 5 IT companies cut a combined 6,981 jobs in FY26.
- **Manufacturing is growing.** In a TeamLease survey, 85% of manufacturing employers planned to hire freshers in the first half of 2026, against 73% of all employers.
- **Lyra's page lists the wrong companies.** Zoho and Freshworks hire only a few students with high cut-offs.
