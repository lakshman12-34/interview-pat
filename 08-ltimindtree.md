## About the name change

- LTIMindtree announced the new name **LTM** on 11 Feb 2026, subject to approvals.
- A third-party report says it took effect on 17 Mar 2026. This date is not verified.
- ltimindtree.com/careers now redirects to ltm.com/careers. That page has no campus details and links to careers.ltimindtree.com.
- **In Lyra, show it as "LTM (formerly LTIMindtree)"** so students recognise it.

## 1. Tracks and entry pay

| Track | Pay | Source / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET)** | ₹4,05,233 a year | College notices for the 2026 batch, the Sep 2026 notice for the 2027 batch, and a July 2025 news report. Found in 4+ sources. |
| **Associate Trainee** (non-engineering) | ₹3,19,000 a year | Referral-only drive for the 2024–25 batches, deadline 13 Feb 2026 |
| Premium track | ₹6.5–9.0 LPA | FACE Prep only. Not confirmed by a second source. |

A search snippet mentioned raises to about ₹5 L or ₹6.5 L for strong performers, but the pages behind it would not load. Treat it as unverified.

## 2. Eligibility

**GET, 2026 batch:**
- **Degrees:** B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech in any branch, MCA, or 5-year integrated M.Sc in CS/IT.
- **Marks:** 60% throughout (Class 10, Class 12, diploma, UG, PG).
- **Backlogs:** none standing.
- **Gap:** 2 years or less.
- **Citizenship:** Indian citizens only.

**GET, 2027 batch** (tech6sense, 21 Sep 2026, the newer source):
- Same degrees, but the cut-off is **65%** throughout.
- Gap still 2 years or less. No bond.

**Associate Trainee:**
- 3-year degrees (B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BCA) with 50% throughout.
- Gap of 2 years or less.
- No LTIMindtree test in the last 6 months.

**Conflicting claim:** FACE Prep says the gap allowed is 1 year, and that some drives allow 1–2 backlogs at the test stage.

## 3. Selection process

1. Online test: work competency (aptitude/reasoning), coding, spoken English, essay, personality
2. Technical interview
3. HR interview

Some candidates also report an **AI interview** before the technical round. This is not confirmed as a standard step.

## 4. Online test pattern

2026-batch campus drive, Jan 2026 (GeeksforGeeks).

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Workplace Competency Test (aptitude/reasoning) | Not stated | 30 min |
| Coding | 2 (easy) | 45 min |
| **SVAR spoken-English test** | Not stated | 20 min |
| Essay writing | 1 | 20 min |
| Personality | Not stated | 15 min |
| **Total** | — | **130 min** |

- **Platform:** reportedly AMCAT (search snippet, page not opened).
- **Another 2026-batch account** (Jan 2026): a 3-hour test with aptitude, technical MCQs (pseudocode, OOP, DBMS) and a voice test (sentence repetition, grammar).

**Prep sites disagree, and some seem to mix in old Mindtree and LTI patterns:**
- FACE Prep (May 2026): about 180 min, with 20 English + 15 quant + 15 logical questions and 4 coding problems in C, Java or Python.
- Another FACE Prep page: 50 Q in 60 min with **0.25 negative marking**. This looks like the old L&T Infotech pattern.
- PrepInsta (2025): 111–112 Q in 130 min, not adaptive, no negative marking, sectional cut-offs of 70–80%.

**Not confirmed for 2026:** whether the test is adaptive, and whether there is negative marking.
**Cut-offs:** not published by the company.

## 5. What the interviews focus on

- **Technical:**
  - A deep dive into your project (for example React or MongoDB, or how you would swap Node.js for Spring Boot).
  - The four pillars of OOP.
  - Basic DSA: removing duplicates from an array, binary vs linear search, time complexity.
  - Writing SQL queries.
  - A client-deadline scenario.
- **HR:** motivation, strengths and weaknesses, relocation, your views on AI.
- **Competition:** one campus reported 24 selections out of 600 students.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **2026 batch:** colleges had to submit student data by 8 Sep 2025. Tests and interviews ran until Jan 2026 (for example 17–23 Jan 2026). FACE Prep says drives usually run from August to February.
- **2027 batch:** college data was due 24 Sep 2026. Individual test links come from EarlyCareer.Hiring@ltm.com.
- **How students apply:** mainly through their college placement cell, plus the careers portal (careers.ltm.com or careers.ltimindtree.com). The Associate Trainee route was by employee referral only.

## 7. Hiring volume

- **FY26:** target of 5,000 freshers (HRKatha, 23 Jul 2025).
- **FY27 (Apr 2026–Mar 2027):** "more than 6,000 freshers", with about 1,300 hired in Q1 FY27 (Business Today, 13 Jul 2026).

## 8. Conflicts and gaps

- **High confidence:**
  - GET pay of ₹4,05,233.
  - No backlogs and a gap of 2 years or less.
  - 60% cut-off for the 2026 batch and 65% for the 2027 batch.
  - The hiring numbers (from news reports).
- **Medium:** the 130-minute test. It rests on one detailed candidate account plus a search snippet.
- **Not found:**
  - Question counts per section for 2026.
  - Whether the 2026 test is adaptive or has negative marking.
  - Official cut-offs.
  - Official details of a premium track.
  - Confirmation of the ₹5 L / ₹6.5 L raises.

## 9. What a Lyra student should practise

- **Marks:** the cut-off rises to 65% for the 2027 batch. Lyra's eligibility rules should use this figure.
- **Aptitude and reasoning:** 30 minutes.
- **Coding:** 2 easy problems in 45 minutes.
- **Spoken English (SVAR):** sentence repetition and grammar. Lyra has no spoken-English practice today.
- **Essay:** 20 minutes.
- **Personality test:** 15 minutes. Lyra's soft-skills scenarios are related practice.
- **Interview:** project deep-dive, OOP, basic DSA, SQL. The AI mock interview covers this.
